0	"Frigidae" is the site of a Roman castellum located 18 km south of Lixus that is mentioned as a station on the Antonine Itinerary.
1	"Kastri 2" is the site of a Late Minoan settlement that is located to the west of Apodoulou, in Rethymno, Crete.
2	"Laodicea on the Lycus": an ancient settlement located near the modern village of Eskihisar (Eski Hissar) in Denizli Province, Turkey.
3	"Magliana Vecchia" is the name of the 40th urban administrative zone of the Comune di Roma. It is located to the southwest of the city center and straddles the Grande Raccordo Anulare (Ring Road) line that circles the city. The compilers of the Barrington Atlas noted that this place includes a "bridge" and a "settlement". In terms of archaeological remains, numerous sites have been identified in and around Magliana Vecchia, including a Roman bridge and several villas.
4	"Sioua" is the Greek reconstruction of an ancient toponym (found in Egyptian sources: S·t-wȝḥ) that encompassed some part of the western end of the Dakhla Oasis in Egypt (certainly the site now known as Deir al-Haggar was included, and possibly also Amheida).
5	(A)Esica, also known as Great Chesters, was a fort on the line of Hadrian's Wall.
6	(B)Aletium (Alezio) was a Messapic center that had ties to the Iapyges, according to Pliny. Saracens destroyed the site ca. A.D. 1000.
7	(H)Atria (modern Adria) was a Venetic and, later, Etruscan settlement. The city itself is likely a sixth century BC foundation.
8	*Argentomagus was a pre-Roman and later Roman settlement in the territory of the Bituriges tribe.
9	*Aurinia/Saturnia was a settlement in Etruria. Dionysius of Halicarnassus claimed it was founded by the Pelasgians. Saturnia received a Roman colony in 183 BC.
10	*Gariann(on)um is the site of a third-century A.D. Roman coastal fort.
11	*Glannoventa was a Roman fort and naval base on the coast of Cumbria.
12	*Letocetum was an important military post near the junction of Watling Street and Icknield Street.
13	*Moridunum (Carmarthen) was the civitas capital of the Demetae in Roman Wales.
14	*Ponte Navata, a late Roman fort on Sibrik hill, Visegrád, Hungary.
15	*Porsulae/Maximianopolis, also known as Mosynopolis, was a Byzantine town along the Via Egnatia in Thrace; its position is close to the modern Greek city of Komotini.
16	*Sedunum was the principal settlement of the Seduni.
17	*Tiddis (or Castellum Tidditanorum) was a Roman city located in the Constantine Province in Algeria.
18	A "Kenchreai in the Troad," mentioned first by Stephanus, is probably to be identified with a settlement of Roman date, located atop the Kayalı Dağ in modern Turkey.

96	A Roman camp with baths and associated settlement were identified at modern Souk El Arbaa in Morocco in the late 19th and 20th centuries on high ground occupied by a qubba called "Sidi Aïssa". 139	A Roman fort is thought to have been established at Lindinis (modern Ilchester) ca. 60 CE. The site protected the crossing of the Fosse Way over the River Yeo.
